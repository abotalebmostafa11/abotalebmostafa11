I am an Associate Professor and Head of the Laboratory of Integrated Digital Solutions at Yugra State University. I hold a Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences degree (Ph.D.) in Theoretical Informatics and Cybernetics.

I develop research software for artificial intelligence, Earth observation, time-series forecasting, and scientific computing. My work connects geospatial workflows, machine learning experiments, and mathematics education.

<h2 id="research-focus">Research focus</h2>

| Area | Work |
| :--- | :--- |
| Earth observation & GIS | Satellite imagery, temporal scene selection, raster processing, and spectral indices in QGIS. |
| Forecasting & machine learning | Time-series models, neural networks, ensemble methods, and hyperparameter experiments. |
| Mathematics & education | Linear algebra, Wolfram Language programs, notebooks, and problems with solutions. |

I supervise project No. 2026-252-01, funded by the Foundation for Scientific and Technological Development of Yugra. The project develops an AI-based geoinformation system for monitoring and forecasting waterlogging and forest-wetland degradation in Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Okrug–Yugra.
